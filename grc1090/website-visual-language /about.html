About Us

Welcome to Crumb&Co., a cozy cafe and bakery located San Luis Obispo! At Crumb&Co., our goal is to bring to you a relaxing and quiet space where you can unwind or get productive with a freshly baked treat. Our menu consists of French pastries and bread, as well as carefully crafted teas and coffee.

Crumb&Co. came from the idea of slowing down and enjoying the simplicity of a cup of coffee. Our goal was to create an environment that feels warm and familiar, fostering a space for rest and connection. What started off as an idea for a cafe quickly expanded to a bakery. Heavily inspired by French patisseries, we worked with bakers to design a menu full of delicately crafted pastries and bread. At Crumb&Co., we value the quality and authenticity of our goods, which is why everything we sell is freshly baked every morning.

Beyond our pastries and coffee, we hope Crumb&Co. becomes a place where people can find comfort in the little things. Whether you're stopping by for a morning coffee, meeting a friend for a pastry, or spending a quiet afternoon studying or working, we want every visit to feel like a small escape from the busyness of everyday life. We believe that the best moments don't always have to be grand; sometimes, they can be found in a warm cup of coffee, a freshly baked pastry, and good company.

As a locally rooted café in San Luis Obispo, we hope to become a part of the community by creating a space that brings people together. We strive to make every detail, from the atmosphere of our café to the ingredients in our menu, feel intentional and thoughtfully put together. At Crumb&Co., we invite you to slow down, stay awhile, and enjoy the simple pleasures of freshly baked goods and a good cup of coffee. We're more than just a café and bakery; we're a little corner of comfort, connection, and warmth.
